01 · The problem

A national home-services franchisor pools every unit's ad-fund contribution into one shared budget. National campaigns go out. Leads come in. But nothing in between connects a specific unit's dollars to that unit's results:

  • Every unit's contribution lands in one pooled national budget, no per-unit tag attached.
  • Campaigns launch and leads arrive, but the link back to whose dollars paid for which lead never gets recorded.
  • When a franchisee asks what their share actually bought, the honest answer is nobody can say.
This isn't hypothetical

Re-Bath's most recently disclosed FDD lists 5+ federal actions and 17+ AAA arbitrations tied to its marketing fund, against roughly $356,000 in average annual ad spend per unit — and none of it comes with a ledger either side can point to.

02 · The fix
Solution
  • A shared, real-time fund ledger both HQ and franchisees read from — not two separate spreadsheets.
  • A campaign launcher that allocates every approved dollar by unit or region before it's spent, not after.
  • A content engine that turns each campaign into localized assets, pushed straight to the units that funded it.
Outcome
  • Every unit sees exactly what was spent on its behalf, the moment it's approved.
  • Inside the same ledger HQ already uses — not a parallel audit trail.
  • Not a better audit. A ledger where the question never comes up.

03 · How Beacon works

Four stages, one shared ledger running underneath all of them. Tap a stage to see what happens inside it.

  • Pool balances across every unit's contribution, live.
  • Vendor rebates and franchisee contributions reconciled in one place.
  • An audit log HQ and franchisees both read — not just HQ.
  • A five-step wizard: type → channels → targeting → budget → review.
  • Every dollar is tagged to a unit or region before approval, not after spend.
  • Lifecycle is tracked end to end: drafted → approved → pushed.
  • Research phase grounds the creative in the unit's actual market.
  • Generation phase includes a Meta Compliance Score before anything ships.
  • Push phase sends the finished asset straight to the units that funded it.
  • Fund Ledger: contribution rate, amount owed, amount paid, status — same numbers HQ sees.
  • Content Library: every asset pushed from HQ, already tied to this unit's spend.
  • No separate request, no waiting on a report — it's just there.
